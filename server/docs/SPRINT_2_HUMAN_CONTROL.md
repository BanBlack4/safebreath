# SafeBreath AI: Sprint 2 — HUMAN CONTROL & CONSENT

## Strengthening User Agency
Users navigating anxiety must never feel trapped by the software.

### Consent Management
*   Easy toggles in settings to withdraw local data processing or BLE access.
*   Clear definitions of who to call during an SOS (Custom Emergency Contact vs. 911).

### Customization
Allowing users to shape their support:
*   Disabling specific haptics if they cause sensory overload.
*   Choosing the intervention color palette (Teal vs. Deep Blue).

### Escalation Previews
Users can "Test" the intervention and SOS flow manually from the settings menu so they know exactly what it looks and feels like before they ever need it in a real panic scenario.
