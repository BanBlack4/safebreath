# SafeBreath AI: Sprint 2 — INTERVENTION EXPLAINABILITY

## Transparent Physiological Feedback
When a prompt appears, it must be clear *why*.

### Alert Explanations
*   Instead of: "CRITICAL HR SPIKE DETECTED"
*   Use: "Notamos un aumento inusual en tu ritmo cardíaco."

### Contextual Reassurance
If the signal is noisy, we don't present it as a certainty.
*   "Parece que hubo una anomalía en las lecturas de tu sensor. ¿Te sientes bien?"

### Avoiding Medical Intimidation
We do not use terms like "Tachycardia", "Arrhythmia", or "Fibrillation". We describe the user's *experience*, not a clinical diagnosis. "Tu pulso está más acelerado de lo habitual" is human and easily understood without panicking the user.
